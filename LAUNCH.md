## Methodology thread

Post 1

> How Buzzword Bingo works:
>
> We attempted all 225 eligible YC Fall 2026 homepages in a pinned community list: 215 scored, 10 unscored. We compared them with 50 systematically selected public-cloud companies (49 scored). No replacement pages.

Post 2

> 50 frozen phrases. Weights 1–3.
>
> Density = weighted phrase hits / homepage words × 100. No LLM in the score. Explicit variants and word boundaries; longest overlaps win. Only saved public homepage text.

Post 3

> Real example: Antropi Robotics has 8 weighted hits across 233 homepage words, giving 3.43 per 100.
>
> Counts describe wording, not whether the product is good or its claims are true.
