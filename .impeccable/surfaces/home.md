Mode: Operate, with Read for methodology. The user has approved the product, stack, data, and visual direction in PLAN.md and the Phase 3 brief. Work directly in code from those decisions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The full eligible YC batch and the systematic public-cloud cohort share the same rulebook. Agentic leads with fraction and share of scored companies; raw mentions stay secondary. A public bingo score sheet makes the comparison understandable before visitors enter a leaderboard. The real sample result, not a startup joke, determines the headline explanation.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground, dark ink, cobalt incumbents, coral startups, restrained green for zero-hit results. Barlow Condensed display lettering and Public Sans reading text. Printed scoreboards, numbered balls, actual bingo grids, clear tab states.

STORY: Read the provisional result, compare both cohorts, inspect a company, and share or play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact masthead, a large headline, and a shared two-column comparison scoreboard. Median density leads; zero-hit percentages and coverage follow. An agentic comparison strip completes the result before the cohort tabs.

FORM: The bingo-hall world and comparison hierarchy are pinned by the approved brief. Code-led data interface. Seed: none, pinned direction. The signature interaction is switching cohort score sheets while sorting real results.

FINISH: Real desktop and mobile verification, final static full-batch screenshots and image provenance. Preserve the approved visual world. Stop for Phase 4 review before public deployment.
